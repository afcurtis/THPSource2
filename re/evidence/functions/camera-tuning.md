Status: confirmed by retail forced calls (23,939 cases of the camera layer, no mismatch)

Native: `src/runtime/camera_kit.hpp`, `camera_tuning.cpp` (generated by `build/p415b/mk_tuning.py` from the disassembly); replay `src/runtime/camera_retail_calls_test.cpp`; probe
`re/gdb/probes/cam_specs_small.py` (group `cms`); fixtures `re/evidence/fixtures/camera-calls-cms*.txt`

## What is here

Each initialiser is a thunk and a body of three stores that put the default of one camera tuning value (a vector of three dwords, a trio of shorts or bytes) into the globals at `0x0055f750..0x0055fa14`, where the
shake and the offsets of the camera modes read it. The bodies hold no branches and call nothing. The names are `Camera_Tuning_0040ED80` and `Camera_TuningThunk_0040ED80` and so on until
`0x0040F0B0`: the address of the body names the pair.

## Method

Forced calls with the target globals poked with random words (the stores overwrite them whatever they held); the replay compares every written byte. 40 cases of each function are enough: the code takes no input.

## Lessons

(1) With no input and no branches a body is fully described by its stores, so the port is generated from the disassembly and the replay is the proof that the generator read each store (the width of a store
is the width of the field). (2) The thunks are separate retail functions: each is replayed through its own forced call.
